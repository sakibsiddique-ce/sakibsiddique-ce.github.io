Journal article

A Deep Semi-Supervised Learning based Damage Identification of a Bridge Using Vehicle-Induced Dynamic Displacement and Multilayer Perceptron Model

Siddique, S., & Rana, S. (2024). IUT Journal of Engineering and Technology (IUT JET), Vol. 19, No. 2, pp. 479–487. Islamic University of Technology, Bangladesh.

Presented at IUT-ICCET 2024 (25–27 April 2025). Proposes a semi-supervised MLP-based framework for detecting bridge structural damage from vehicle-induced dynamic displacement signals, addressing the scarcity of labeled damage data common in real-world SHM applications.

Undergraduate thesis

Bridge Damage Identification Using Bi-LSTM

B.Sc. Thesis, Department of Civil Engineering, BUET (2024). Supervisor: Dr. Shohel Rana.

Developed a Bi-directional LSTM model for structural damage detection using vibration response data, capturing temporal dependencies to improve sensitivity to subtle damage patterns.

In progress

Damage localization from multi-sensor vibration data

Follow-up manuscript, in preparation.

Extends the detection framework above from binary damage classification toward locating damage within the structure, using spatial information across multiple sensors.

Road Damage Detection Using Deep Learning (RDD2022 benchmark)

Supervisor: Dr. Celia Shahnaz. Target journal: IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems.

YOLOv8-based multi-national road damage detection and classification framework, benchmarked on the RDD2022 dataset (47,420 road images, 6 countries), targeting improvement over the YOLOv5 baseline (mAP@0.5 = 0.563) reported in Construction and Building Materials (Elsevier, Q1, 2024). Approved by Dr. Celia Shahnaz.

Facility Management Data Analytics (FMUCD dataset)

Reviewed by: Dr. Kahyun Jeon, Illinois Institute of Technology

Exploratory data analysis and quantitative diagnostics on a 3.7-million-record facility maintenance dataset spanning 12 North American universities. Produced a reproducible analysis notebook and a research proposal on predictive maintenance classification, independently reviewed and positively assessed by Dr. Kahyun Jeon (IIT).
