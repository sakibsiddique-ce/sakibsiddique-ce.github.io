Publications

Journal article, thesis, and work in progress

Journal article

A Deep Semi-Supervised Learning based Damage Identification of a Bridge Using Vehicle-Induced Dynamic Displacement and Multilayer Perceptron Model

Siddique, S., & Rana, S. (2024). IUT Journal of Engineering and Technology, Vol. 19, No. 2, pp. 479–487. Islamic University of Technology, Dhaka, Bangladesh. Presented at IUT-ICCET.

A semi-supervised Multilayer Perceptron framework for detecting bridge structural damage from vehicle-induced dynamic displacement signals, addressing the scarcity of labeled damage data that limits data-driven SHM in practice.

PDF

Thesis

Bridge Damage Identification Using Bi-LSTM

B.Sc. thesis, Department of Civil Engineering, BUET, 2024 · Supervisor: Dr. Shohel Rana

A Bi-directional LSTM model that detects and locates structural damage by analyzing differences in vibration data, trained on real-world datasets to improve accuracy and combining structural engineering principles with deep learning.

In preparation

Damage localization from multi-sensor vibration data

Follow-up manuscript to the published detection framework

Extends binary damage classification toward locating damage within the structure using spatial information across multiple sensors.

Road damage detection using deep learning on the RDD2022 benchmark

Supervisor: Dr. Celia Shahnaz · target journal: IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems

YOLOv8-based multi-national road damage detection and classification, benchmarked on 47,420 road images from six countries, targeting improvement over the YOLOv5 baseline (mAP@0.5 = 0.563) reported in Construction and Building Materials (Elsevier, Q1, 2024).

Predictive maintenance classification on the FMUCD dataset

Research proposal and analysis notebook · reviewed by Dr. Kahyun Jeon, Illinois Institute of Technology

Exploratory analysis and quantitative diagnostics on 3.7 million facility maintenance records from twelve North American universities, forming the basis of a proposal on predictive maintenance classification.
