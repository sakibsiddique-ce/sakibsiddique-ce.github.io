I am a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at Daffodil International University, Dhaka, and a graduate of the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where I completed my B.Sc. in Civil Engineering (Major: Structural Engineering, Minor: Transportation Engineering) in July 2024 with a CGPA of 3.67/4.00.

My research sits at the intersection of structural engineering and machine learning, focused on structural health monitoring (SHM) — using data-driven methods to detect and eventually localize damage in bridges and other infrastructure from vibration response data. I am currently applying for PhD positions to continue this work.

Background

The motivation behind my research is straightforward: Bangladesh's bridge network is aging and under increasing stress from traffic density, environmental exposure, and climatic risk, while conventional visual inspection is slow, subjective, and blind to internal damage. My undergraduate thesis, supervised by Dr. Shohel Rana at BUET, addressed this with a deep learning framework for bridge damage identification using vehicle-induced dynamic displacement data, published as a first-author paper at IUT-ICCET 2024. I am currently extending that work toward damage localization using multi-sensor vibration data.

Alongside SHM, I have a secondary interest in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), and I teach Structural Analysis, Engineering Mechanics, Bridge Design, Python Programming, and AutoCAD at DIU, where I also serve on the Curriculum Committee supporting the department's outcome-based education and accreditation work.
