Sakib Siddique

Structural Health Monitoring · Machine Learning for Infrastructure

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at Daffodil International University, Dhaka. I completed my B.Sc. in Civil Engineering at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) in July 2024, with a major in Structural Engineering and a minor in Transportation Engineering.

Email CV Publications GitHub

My work sits between structural engineering and machine learning. The problem I keep returning to is this: when the only thing available is noisy, indirect sensor data, how do you tell reliably what is happening inside a structure? My undergraduate thesis approached it as a damage detection problem on a bridge, using vehicle-induced vibration response and a semi-supervised deep learning model trained where labeled damage cases are scarce. I am now extending that work from detecting damage to locating it.

Two further projects apply the same data-driven approach elsewhere in civil infrastructure: a YOLOv8-based road damage detection framework benchmarked on RDD2022, and a predictive maintenance analysis of a 3.7-million-record facility maintenance dataset. I am currently applying for fully funded PhD positions in these areas.

Research interests

  • Structural health monitoring and vibration-based damage detection
  • Damage localization from multi-sensor response data
  • Deep learning for civil infrastructure — MLP, Bi-LSTM, and object detection models
  • Learning under scarce labels: semi-supervised and physics-informed approaches
  • Intelligent transportation systems and automated road condition assessment
  • Predictive maintenance and facility asset analytics
  • Risk assessment frameworks for aging bridge networks in Bangladesh
